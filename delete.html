CallGuard - How to Delete Your Data

Local Data (on your phone)

All CallGuard data stored on your phone — including your blacklist, call records, voicemail messages, and settings — is deleted automatically when you uninstall the app.

  1. Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → CallGuard
  2. Tap Uninstall
  3. All local data is permanently deleted. No recovery possible.

Cloud Backup (Google Drive, PRO only)

If you used the PRO cloud sync feature, your backup is stored in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. To delete it:

Option A - In the app (before uninstalling):

  1. Open CallGuard → Settings → Cloud Sync
  2. Tap "Delete cloud backup"
  3. Confirm deletion

Option B - Via Google account (after uninstalling):

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions
  2. Find "CallGuard" in the list
  3. Click "Remove access" — this revokes Drive access and you may then delete the appDataFolder contents from Google Drive

What Data We Hold

CallGuard operates on a zero-server principle. We do not operate any servers that store your data. The only data that ever leaves your phone is:

  • Your encrypted backup file in your own Google Drive (PRO only, only if you enable it)

We retain no data on our side. Once you delete the local app data and/or the Drive backup, no copies remain.
