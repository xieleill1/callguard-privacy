CallGuard Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Our Core Commitment

CallGuard is built on a zero-collection, zero-server, zero-account principle. All your data stays on your phone. We operate no servers that receive your data.

What We Collect

We collect no personal information. Specifically:

  • Contacts: Read locally on your device only, to identify whether an incoming call is from a contact. Contact data is never uploaded or transmitted.
  • Call Audio: When screening unknown calls, the microphone is used solely for on-device speech recognition (5–8 seconds). Audio is not saved, not uploaded, and not transmitted. It is discarded immediately after recognition.
  • Call Records: Blocked-call logs and voicemail messages are stored only in the local database on your phone.

Network Usage

The app uses the network in the following cases. All transmissions are encrypted via HTTPS:

  1. Downloading Speech Recognition Models: On first use, speech recognition model files are downloaded from the official Vosk website (alphacephei.com) — approximately 48 MB for Chinese, 42 MB for English. Download only; no data is uploaded.
  2. Updating Greeting Templates: On launch, the app fetches the latest greeting templates from our public GitHub repository (github.com/xieleill1/callguard-templates). Download only, no upload — none of your data is sent to GitHub.
  3. Google Play Subscriptions: PRO subscription purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment information is handled by Google; we do not access it.
  4. Google Drive Cloud Sync (PRO Feature): If you subscribe to PRO and sign in with a Google account, your blacklist, settings, and voicemail metadata are backed up to a hidden folder (appDataFolder) in your own Google Drive. Only CallGuard can read and write this folder. You may delete cloud backups at any time via "Settings → Cloud Sync" in the app.

Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. If you used Drive cloud sync, you may delete cloud backups via "Settings → Cloud Sync" in the app, or revoke CallGuard's Drive access in your Google account settings.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not collect information from children.

Policy Updates

We will notify you in the app when this policy is updated. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
